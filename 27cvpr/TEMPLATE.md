The 2026 format is two-column US Letter, with 10-point main text and at most
eight pages of main content including figures and tables; additional
reference-only pages are allowed. The appendix is built as a separate
supplement. The results remain placeholders from the original working draft.

When the official 2027 kit is released, compare its author instructions,
replace the two upstream style files as needed, update this provenance record,
and recompile both documents. Changing `confYear` alone does not establish
compliance with the future 2027 guidelines.
