\subsection{Experimental setup}
\label{sec:experiments}

MIMIC-CXR~\citep{johnson2019mimiccxr} supplies radiographs, finding labels,
and longitudinal image--report pairs; MIMIC-CXR-VQA supplies questions and
answer sets. A global patient holdout removes conflicting records from
lower-priority splits. The resulting training pools contain 12,818 classification
images, 3,825 segmentation images, 281,092 VQA questions, and 14,868 temporal
pairs. Validation pools contain 160 images, 249 images, 72,821 questions, and
227 pairs, respectively. Reversing temporal pairs produces 29,736 directed
training examples. Reports are used for temporal representation learning but
withheld from all current-task inputs; EHR variables are not model inputs.

We compare native zero-shot checkpoints with independently trained Qwen3.5-0.8B
models with and without slot conditioning. Both trained arms share task pools,
effective batch sizes, losses, and optimizer-update counts. The current paired
run allocates 1,350 updates per arm on two GPUs, with per-rank batches of 128
for classification, segmentation, and temporal pairs, and 64 for VQA, without
gradient accumulation. Training and evaluation are incomplete; matched updates
do not imply matched GPU hours. Final testing selects 353 classification images
and a fixed 300-question VQA subset, rather than the full VQA test split.
Segmentation is trained but its final evaluation is currently disabled; its
columns remain pending. Results are single-seed point estimates.
