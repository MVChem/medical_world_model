\subsection{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experiments}

\paragraph{Datasets.}
We conduct experiments on the MIMIC datasets and two longitudinal glioma MRI cohorts. MIMIC-CXR provides chest radiographs and associated reports, with repeated examinations supporting temporal state prediction. MIMIC-CXR-VQA supplies question--answer annotations for evaluating clinical image understanding, while MIMIC-IV provides linked hospital admission records and clinical information. We additionally include MU-Glioma-Post and UCSF-ALPTDG, which contain longitudinal multi-sequence brain MRI, tumor segmentation annotations, and clinical metadata. These cohorts extend the evaluation of state representations and temporal evolution to a complementary imaging modality and disease setting. All observations from the same patient are assigned to the same data split. Dataset statistics, cohort construction, and preprocessing details are provided in the appendix.

\paragraph{Compared Methods.}
We compare MedWorld-JEPA with representative visual representation models (DINOv2, RAD-DINO, and CheXWorld) and vision--language models (Qwen3.5-9B and MedGemma-1.5-4B). For downstream adaptation, we extract intermediate features and attach task-specific decoders. Adapted models use the same training splits and number of optimization steps for a controlled comparison. For future-state prediction, visual models are equipped with horizon-conditioned predictors. We additionally include direct Qwen-based forecasting and a persistence baseline that assumes no change from the current state.
