{\Large\bfseries Table 1 results: future prediction}\par
Predict the unobserved follow-up from the current radiograph, current report,
source-available clinical history, and requested horizon. Results available on
15 September 2026 are entered below. The public Qwen baseline and the three
completed legacy forecast conditions use Qwen3.5-9B. A new native-image-conditioned 4+4 comparison is in progress.

\input{plans/tables/table1_future_plan}

\textbf{Scoring interface.}
For each finding, score the fixed candidate answers \texttt{Yes} and \texttt{No}
from the VLM's conditional token likelihoods:
\[
 s_k=\frac{\exp(\ell_{\mathrm{Yes},k})}
 {\exp(\ell_{\mathrm{Yes},k})+\exp(\ell_{\mathrm{No},k})}.
\]
Here $\ell$ is the candidate answer's log-likelihood at the same answer boundary.
Trained forecasters use finding heads. AP/AUROC assess ranking; Brier/ECE assess
the same raw scores as probabilities. These are distinct training regimes.

\textbf{Evaluation status.}
The 297-pair reference masks are shared. Transition F1 averages supported
finding-specific onset/resolution events. All entered GREEN scores cover all
297 pairs. Direction labels for this cohort remain unreviewed; scores from the
separate 82-pair pilot are excluded. The bottom three rows share a newly trained
9B Stage~1 initialization and have completed matched forecast training and
evaluation. Historical 0.8B scores are retained in the experiment records.
The 9B slot adaptation
uses final-language-layer queries, distinct from the proposed multi-depth
fusion/vision model. The new comparison retains native current-image and history inputs at the decoder and adds predicted future slots; its results are not yet available. CheXagent inference and scoring are complete; the prior's
transition readout remains pending. Source paths, hashes and full-precision values are saved in
\texttt{tables/results\_20260914.json}.

{\Large\bfseries Table 2 results: six downstream tasks}\par
Evaluate current-image understanding, language readout, localization, and
reconstruction. The six task families are classification, standard VQA,
current report generation, phrase grounding, organ segmentation, and spatial
$4\times$ super-resolution.

\input{plans/tables/table2_downstream_plan}

\textbf{Data.}
Filled entries use MIMIC-CXR classification, reports and synthetic SR, CXAS
pseudo-labels, and external Montgomery human lung masks. All filled dense-task
rows use the 4,096-image training cohort. The 18,708-image runs are reported
separately in the experiment records. PSNR/SSIM use the same valid image region.

\textbf{Remaining evaluations.}
Official MIMIC-CXR-VQA, MS-CXR phrase grounding and matched six-task adaptation
of the full proposed model remain pending. The Qwen3.5-9B native row reports
zero-shot classification/reporting; its separate dense rows train heads on
four fixed tokens from the frozen vision tower. All filled dense rows share
data and updates, but actual GPU hours and pretraining differ. The old
report-assisted $2\times$ SR and separate two-epoch VLM adaptation pilots are
not scores for the full six-task model.
